# WeCrew InfraAsset — product brief

Positioning: **AI Infrastructure Intelligence Platform** for on-prem, cloud and edge.

InfraAsset answers: what we own, where it lives, what depends on it, what is unhealthy,
what capacity remains, what fails together, and where a workload should run.

The control plane is a dense, desktop-first operations product (not a marketing dashboard).
Every AI recommendation is required to cite assets and metrics.
